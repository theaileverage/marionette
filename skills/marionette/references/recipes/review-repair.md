# review-repair

Version: 1

Trigger: When evidence identifies a need for independent review or a targeted correction.

Define the question the review should answer and the authoritative artifact revision. Choose independence appropriate to the risk and available evidence. Report concrete defects, their consequences and a reproducible check; do not request changes solely to mirror personal style. Turn actionable findings into scoped repair work. Re-run affected checks and integrated verification against the final artifact. Preserve disagreements with their evidence. Avoid adding a mandatory review layer when existing evaluation already answers the question.

This is optional guidance. The lead may adapt the method while preserving the user objective, permissions, evidence and concurrent work.

Example: A reviewer reproduces a missing retry guard. Assign the guard repair, rerun that reproduction, then evaluate the integrated result against the original contract.
